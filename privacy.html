Privacy Policy

PhimThai - Thai Phonetic Keyboard

Last Updated: January 2025

← Back to Home
TL;DR: PhimThai collects zero data. Everything happens on your device. We never see what you type, where you type it, or any other information about you.

1. Information We Collect

None.

PhimThai does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data. We have no servers, no analytics, and no tracking mechanisms.

2. How PhimThai Works

PhimThai is a keyboard input method that:

  • Runs entirely on your device (iPhone, iPad, Android phone, or Mac)
  • Does not require an internet connection
  • Does not transmit any data over the network
  • Does not access your contacts, photos, location, or any other data

All text processing happens locally using the Thai dictionary bundled with the app. Nothing you type ever leaves your device.

3. Data Storage

PhimThai stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Word count (free tier only): The number of Thai words you've typed today, used to enforce the 300 words/day limit for free users. This counter resets at midnight and is never transmitted anywhere.
  • Premium status: Whether you've purchased the premium unlock. This is stored locally and verified through the App Store/Play Store when needed.
  • User preferences: Any keyboard settings you've configured (if applicable in future versions).

This data is stored using standard device storage APIs (UserDefaults on iOS/macOS, SharedPreferences on Android) and is never synchronized, backed up to cloud services, or transmitted to any server.

4. Keyboard Access

iOS/iPadOS:

PhimThai does not request "Full Access" permission. This means:

  • The keyboard cannot access the internet
  • The keyboard cannot access your other apps' data
  • The keyboard runs in a completely sandboxed environment

Android:

PhimThai only requests standard keyboard permissions required to function as an input method. It does not request internet access, location, contacts, or any other sensitive permissions.

macOS:

PhimThai is installed as a standard Input Method and operates with minimal system permissions. It does not access the internet, file system (beyond its own resources), or any other apps' data.

5. Third-Party Services

PhimThai uses the following third-party services:

In-App Purchases (iOS/Android):

When you purchase the premium unlock, the transaction is processed entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play Store. We do not have access to your payment information. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy or Google's Privacy Policy for information about how they handle payment data.

No Other Third Parties:

PhimThai does not integrate with any analytics services, advertising networks, crash reporting tools, or any other third-party services.

6. Analytics & Tracking

PhimThai contains no analytics, no tracking, and no telemetry. We don't know:

  • How often you use the keyboard
  • What words you type
  • Which apps you use it in
  • Your device information
  • Your location
  • Anything else about your usage

7. Data Sharing

Since we don't collect any data, we have nothing to share. PhimThai does not sell, rent, or share any user information with third parties.

8. Children's Privacy

PhimThai is suitable for users of all ages. Since we don't collect any data, there are no special considerations for children's privacy. We comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) by not collecting any information from users of any age.

9. Data Security

The most secure data is data that doesn't exist. Since PhimThai doesn't collect or transmit any data, there's no risk of your typing data being intercepted, stolen, or compromised.

The word count and premium status stored locally on your device are protected by your device's standard security mechanisms (encryption at rest, app sandboxing, etc.).

10. Your Rights

Under various privacy laws (GDPR, CCPA, etc.), you have rights regarding your personal data. Since PhimThai doesn't collect any personal data:

  • Right to Access: There is no data to access.
  • Right to Deletion: There is no data to delete.
  • Right to Portability: There is no data to export.
  • Right to Opt-Out: There is nothing to opt out of.

If you want to reset your local word count or premium status, you can simply uninstall and reinstall the app.

11. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change how PhimThai handles data, we will update this privacy policy and notify users through app updates. The "Last Updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.

We are committed to maintaining PhimThai as a privacy-respecting app and will not introduce data collection without explicit user consent and transparency.

12. International Users

PhimThai can be used anywhere in the world. Since all processing happens on your device and no data is transmitted, there are no cross-border data transfer issues.
