Summary: PhimThai works entirely offline and collects no data. We have no servers and no way to see what you type.

Local Storage

PhimThai stores the following on your device only:

  • Word count: Daily counter for the free tier limit. Resets at midnight.
  • Premium status: Whether you've unlocked premium, verified through the App Store/Play Store.

This data never leaves your device. To reset it, uninstall and reinstall the app.

Keyboard Permissions

iOS: PhimThai does not request "Full Access", so it cannot access the internet or other apps' data.

Android: PhimThai only requests standard keyboard permissions. No internet, location, or contacts access.

In-App Purchases

Premium purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We don't have access to your payment information. See Apple's or Google's privacy policies for details.

Children's Privacy

PhimThai is suitable for all ages and complies with COPPA by not collecting any information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last Updated" date and notify users through app updates.
